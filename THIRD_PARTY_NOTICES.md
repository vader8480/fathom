projects below. Their licences and notices follow.

## Rackula

Fathom's rack device catalog (`fathom/library/rackula/catalog.json`) and the
device images under `fathom/static/rack-images/` were generated by
`tools/rackula_catalog.py` from Rackula
(<https://github.com/RackulaLives/Rackula>, commit
`9c7af6dd28209a776aebdb92080bcf2916cd29e6`). Several rack features in
Fathom (rack accessories, shelves, category colours, catalog palette, Rackula
layout import) are modelled on Rackula's behaviour and re-implemented from
scratch; no Rackula source code is included.

## NetBox devicetype-library

Rackula's device type definitions (manufacturer, model, height, depth,
airflow and the like), and so the entries of Fathom's rack catalog, come from
the NetBox community devicetype-library
(<https://github.com/netbox-community/devicetype-library>). That data is
dedicated to the public domain under the Creative Commons Zero v1.0 Universal
(CC0 1.0) Public Domain Dedication
(<https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>): the authors have waived
all copyright and related rights to the extent possible under law. No
attribution is required; we credit the NetBox community for maintaining it.

## Device images (vastoholic/draw-io)

Many of the device images in `fathom/static/rack-images/` originate from
vastoholic's Draw.io rack shape library (<https://github.com/vastoholic/draw-io>),
by way of Rackula and the NetBox devicetype-library. Per that repository's
README the files are "freely shareable to anyone who wishes to use them in any
project", with no attribution required; we credit the author nonetheless.
Product names and appearances in the images belong to their manufacturers.
